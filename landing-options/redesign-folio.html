The Invitation

Texas Dawah began with a handful of students at the University of Texas who believed something simple: that the truth is best offered, never forced. We set a table on the Drag, opened our books, and started answering the questions people actually carry, about God, about Jesus, about suffering and meaning and why any of it should matter.

Years later the table is still there. So is the conviction behind it. We are not here to win arguments. We are here to be the reason someone, one afternoon between classes, felt the mercy of Islam for the first time, and decided to look closer.

Everything we do begins with a conversation and ends, we hope, in understanding. You are welcome at the table, whatever you believe.

وَقُل رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

In the margin. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and beautiful instruction. Wisdom is knowing what to say. Beautiful character is the reason anyone stays to listen.
