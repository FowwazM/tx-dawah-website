The table on the Drag.

A photographic record of Texas Dawah, where students at the University of Texas share the message of Islam, one conversation at a time.

Fig. 01 · Who we are

We are students. The truth is our invitation.

Texas Dawah is a student-led community at the University of Texas. Each week we carry a table to the busiest corner of campus, open our books, and answer the questions people actually carry: about God, about Jesus, about suffering and meaning.

We are not here to win arguments. We are here to be the reason someone looked closer at Islam, and found mercy in it. You are welcome at the table, whatever you believe.

Offered with wisdom and beautiful character.
