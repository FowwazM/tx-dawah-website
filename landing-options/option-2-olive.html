Our mission

We invite our neighbors to Islam the way it was meant to be shared: with knowledge, patience, and beautiful character, on the campuses and street corners of Austin.

From a weekly table on the Drag to interfaith dinners and a free course on the evidence for Islam, everything we do begins with a sincere conversation, never a lecture.

What we do

Many doors, one welcome.

Some begin with a book, some with a meal, some with a hard question. Every one of them begins with people.
