Our mission

Share the truth with wisdom and beautiful character.

Texas Dawah is a student community at the University of Texas at Austin. We set a table on the Drag, open our books, and answer the questions people actually carry: about God, about Jesus, about suffering and meaning. The truth, we believe, is best offered, never imposed.

We are not here to win arguments. We are here to be the reason someone, one afternoon between classes, looked closer at Islam and found mercy in it. Whatever you believe, you are welcome at the table.
