The truth, offered freely. Never forced.

We are a student-led Muslim community at the University of Texas, sharing the message of Islam in Austin with knowledge, patience, and beautiful character. We set a table on the Drag, open our books, and answer the questions people actually carry. You are welcome here, whatever you believe.

What we do

Every program is a different door into the same conversation. Some are for the curious who arrive with questions. Some are for Muslims who want to learn to invite well. All of them are free, and all of them are open.

01

Dawah tabling

Weekly

Free books, cold water, and an honest answer to any question, set up in the busiest corner of campus.

West Mall, the Drag
02

Dawah training

Thursdays, 8 PM

A weekly circle where students learn to share Islam with confidence and care. No experience asked of you, only willingness.

1906 Nueces Street
03

Interfaith iftars

Each Ramadan

We break bread with neighbors of every faith and let the table do what arguments rarely can.

Nueces Mosque

Book club and lectures

Slow reading on faith and doubt, with visiting scholars. A recent guest series: "What did Jesus really say?"
