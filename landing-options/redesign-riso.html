Who we are

Texas Dawah is a student-led crew sharing the message of Islam with wisdom and beautiful character. We are not here to win arguments. We are here to be the reason someone looked closer, and found mercy in it.

Pull up a chair, whoever you are. Bring your doubts and your questions. The table is open to everyone, and everything on it is free.

with peace, the Texas Dawah crew
