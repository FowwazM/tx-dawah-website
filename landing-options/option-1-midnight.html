Who we are

We invite people to Islam the way it was meant to be shared, with knowledge, patience, and beautiful character, right here on the streets and campuses of Austin.

From weekly tabling on the Drag to interfaith dinners and a free course on the evidence for Islam, everything we do begins with a sincere conversation.
