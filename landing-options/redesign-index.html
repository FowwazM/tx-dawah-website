Preface
Austin,
Texas

Texas Dawah began with a handful of students who believed the truth is best offered, never imposed. We set a table on the Drag, opened our books, and started answering the questions people actually carry: about God, about Jesus, about suffering and meaning.

We are not here to win arguments. We are here to be the reason someone, between classes, looked closer at Islam and found mercy in it. Every program is a different door into the same conversation, and you are welcome at the table whatever you believe.
