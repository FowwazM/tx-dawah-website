01Who we are
A student, passing by

So what is Texas Dawah, actually?

Texas Dawah

We are students who set a table, and answer.

We are a student-led group at the University of Texas, and dawah just means invitation. We share the message of Islam the way we would want it shared with us: with knowledge, patience, and good character. No pressure, no performance.

So we set up where people already walk, on the West Mall and the Drag, open our books, and try to give an honest answer to whatever anyone brings us.

On the wordدَعْوَةDa’wah: an invitation, a calling toward. Not a counting of heads.
02The week
A visitor

What do you actually do all week?

Texas Dawah

More than you would guess for one folding table.

Some of it is for Muslims who want to grow into the work. Most of it is open to anyone who is curious. A normal week looks like this:

  • Tabling on the West Mall, most days, with free books and cold water.
  • Dawah training, Thursdays at 8 PM, 1906 Nueces: learning to invite with wisdom, not argument.
  • Interfaith iftars, every Ramadan, where the table does what debate cannot.
  • Book club and a guest lecture, monthly, with visiting scholars.
In the marginMost of it open to anyone curious. None of it owed by anyone.
03On intention
Someone at the table

Isn't this just trying to convert me?

Texas Dawah

We offer. We do not impose.

Honestly, no, not the way you mean it. We are not counting anyone, and we are not keeping score. The Qur'an itself says there is no compulsion in religion, and we take that to heart.

Our part is to share what we have found true and beautiful, clearly and kindly, then leave the rest to you and to God. If you walk off with a free book and a better question than you arrived with, that is a good afternoon for us.

The verse behind itلَا إِكْرَاهَ فِي الدِّينِThere is no compulsion in religion. (Qur’an 2:256)
04The hard one
A friend of a friend

If God is merciful, why is there suffering?

Texas Dawah

The honest answer is that we sit with that one too.

It is the oldest question there is, and it deserves better than a slogan. The short version we hold to: this life is not the whole of the story, pain can carry a meaning we cannot always see from inside it, and mercy is not the absence of hardship but God's nearness within it.

That deserves more room than a table allows, so rather than pretend it fits in a sentence, we will point you to where we wrote it out: If God is merciful, why is there suffering?

Okay. Where do I actually start?

Texas Dawah

With one free courseاقْرَأْ, and an open chair.

Begin with "Why Islam Is True", a free six-part course you can take at your own pace. It walks through the evidence plainly, made for the curious and the skeptical alike. After that, or instead of it, just come find the table. You do not owe us a thing, and you never will.
